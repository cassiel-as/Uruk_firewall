  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  LAW 1 · ART · FREQUENCY
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  The dominant metaphor of education is the vessel and the
  liquid — knowledge as a substance that exists independently
  of the learner; teaching as the act of pouring it in; testing
  as measuring how much remains. This metaphor is embedded in
  vocabulary: 'delivering' curriculum, 'absorbing' material,
  'retaining' information, 'filling' knowledge gaps. The learner
  is aesthetically framed as passive container, not active
  constructor. Every classroom layout, lecture format, and
  examination design reinforces this spatial metaphor.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  LAW 3 · PHYSICS · COST
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  The brain does not store information — it updates probabilistic
  models. Passive reception without prediction error produces
  minimal synaptic consolidation. The Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve
  is not a failure of individual effort; it is the nervous system
  executing its correct function: discarding low-utility inputs
  that generated no prediction error. The mechanism gets worse,
  not better, with scale — larger classrooms, longer lectures,
  higher curriculum density all increase passive reception, all
  accelerate neural discard. This operates below pedagogy.
  It operates at thermodynamics.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  LAW 2 · Psychology:
  The institutional resistance is infrastructural, not
  ideological. Two hundred years of career pathways, teacher
  training, curriculum design frameworks, examination boards,
  university admissions, and employer credential requirements
  are load-bearing on the assumption that learning = measurable
  information acquisition. The defence line is not in any
  individual mind — it is in the coordination problem between
  all minds simultaneously.

  LAW 4 · Chemistry:
  Cross-domain damage across three invisible ledgers: (1) public
  health — chronic learned helplessness is a documented
  precursor to depression and anxiety; (2) labour market —

  (3) democratic capacity — PISA critical thinking decline maps
  directly onto declines in institutional trust and
  susceptibility to misinformation.

  LAW 5 · Science:
  Predictive processing as the physical basis of learning is
  established neuroscience (Friston, Clark, 2010s). Spaced
  repetition, interleaving, retrieval practice, and desirable
  difficulty have decades of replication. What does not exist:
  large-scale longitudinal data on outcomes under genuinely
  restructured pedagogical systems — not scientific uncertainty,
  unfunded certainty.

  Surface: 'Teach more, test more, improve scores.'
  Mid-layer: 'Learning = information transfer.'
  Deep: 'The mind is a storage device.'
  Locke's tabula rasa (1689) became the philosophical foundation
  of Enlightenment pedagogy before neuroscience existed. The
  Prussian compulsory schooling model (1819) institutionalised
  this metaphor at civilisational scale, designed explicitly to
  produce standardised, predictable outputs: literate soldiers

  The school building still faces forward.

[ PHASE 07 — OUTPUT CALCULATION ]

  Scenario: Redefine the primary educational metric from
  information reproduction (test score) to prediction model
  updating (demonstrated reasoning under novel conditions) —
  applied to one national system with full credential and
  assessment restructuring.

  Individual level:
  A learner encounters material designed to violate their
  existing predictions — not to fill a gap, but to break a
  wrong model. Confusion becomes the signal that learning is
  occurring, not the signal that teaching has failed.
  Passive reception becomes physically impossible as the
  primary mode — not by policy, by design.

  System level:
  Teacher training reorients from content delivery to prediction
  error engineering. Examination design shifts from reproduction
  under time pressure to transfer under novel conditions.
  Credential markets face the same restructuring pressure that
  technology hiring has already begun applying: demonstrated
  capability replaces certified exposure.

  Civilisational scale:
  Current trajectory:  PISA reasoning scores declining.
                       Adolescent mental health crisis
                       accelerating. Credential inflation
                       compressing labour market access.
                       Democratic reasoning capacity
                       declining — unmeasured and unfunded.
  Inverted trajectory: Lower bound unknown — no national system
                       has produced the data. Laboratory ceiling
                       (60–80%) is the floor, not the ceiling.

  What is NOT required:
  No new neuroscience. No new technology.
  No increase in per-pupil expenditure.
  No new teacher recruitment.
  No destruction of existing institutions.
  Required: one redefinition — Learning success is not
  information reproduced on demand. It is a predictive model
  demonstrably updated by contact with a reality that violated
  its prior state. Everything else follows from this single
  change in the definition of the outcome being measured.
