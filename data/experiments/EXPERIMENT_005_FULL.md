[ PHASE 03 — EIGHT-LAW INPUT MATRIX ]

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  LAW 3 · PHYSICS · COST
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  From an evolutionary biology perspective, negative
  emotional states are functional signals — they
  carry information about the organism's relationship
  with its environment. Depression signals that the
  environment is depleting rather than nourishing.
  Anxiety signals the presence of unresolved threat.
  Grief signals the loss of an attachment resource.
  These signals are costly (they consume energy,
  impair function) precisely because the cost
  compels the organism to act on the information
  they carry. Suppressing the signal with medication
  reduces the cost of the signal without addressing
  the information it carries. The environmental
  condition that generated the signal persists.
  The organism continues to be depleted.
  This is the physical mechanism of high recurrence
  rates: the signal is quieted, the cause continues
  to operate, the signal re-emerges. Medication
  manages the cost of the signal. It does not
  process the information the signal is transmitting.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  LAW 6 · PHILOSOPHY · LEGISLATION
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  The chemical imbalance model rests on a deep
  philosophical assumption: that the mind is
  a biochemical machine, and that psychological
  suffering is a malfunction of that machine's
  chemistry. This is the Cartesian mind-body model
  applied to psychiatry — the mind is reducible
  to its physical substrate, and fixing the
  substrate fixes the mind. This assumption
  predates neuroscience by three centuries.
  It was incorporated into psychiatric practice
  not because the evidence supported it, but
  because it arrived at the moment (1952–1970s)
  when psychiatry was seeking to establish itself
  as a branch of medicine with biological
  legitimacy. The model provided medical credibility.
  The evidence for its mechanism came second —
  and, as the 2022 Moncrieff review established,
  was never conclusively established.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  LAW 1 · Art:
  "Chemical imbalance" is a machine metaphor applied
  to mind. It makes suffering feel technical and
  fixable — a broken part that can be replaced.
  This narrative reduces stigma (suffering is not
  weakness, it is chemistry) but simultaneously
  removes agency (suffering is not information,
  it is malfunction). Both effects serve the
  pharmaceutical market.

  LAW 2 · Psychology:
  The chemical imbalance model offers patients
  a non-agentic explanation for their suffering —
  it is not their life, their relationships,
  their environment. It is their brain.
  This is psychologically protective in the short
  term and psychologically disempowering in the
  long term: it removes the patient from the
  causal chain of their own recovery.

  LAW 4 · Chemistry:
  Long-term SSRI use produces neurobiological
  adaptations — receptor downregulation —
  that make the brain increasingly dependent on
  the medication to maintain baseline function.
  Discontinuation syndrome affects ~50% of
  long-term users. The treatment produces a
  biological dependency that can outlast
  the original condition. This transformation
  cost is not included in the prescribing
  framework's risk accounting.

  LAW 5 · Science:
  The serotonin deficiency hypothesis has
  never been directly confirmed. Indirect
  evidence (SSRIs increase serotonin and
  reduce depression symptoms in some patients)
  was used to infer the mechanism, but
  correlation does not establish causation.
  The 2022 Moncrieff systematic review of
  17 studies found no consistent support.
  This is not scientific uncertainty —
  it is a 70-year clinical framework built
  on an unconfirmed mechanism.

  LAW 7 · Geography:
  Depression rates are highest in countries
  with weakest social safety nets, highest
  inequality, and most adverse childhood
  experience rates. The geographic distribution
  maps social determinants, not neurochemical
  variation. The treatment (medication) is
  delivered at the individual level while
  the causes operate at the structural and
  geographic level.

  LAW 8 · Religion:
  The medical model of mental illness has
  acquired ritual properties: diagnosis,
  prescription, compliance. The ritual
  provides containment for suffering that
  would otherwise require social and structural
  responses. Pharmaceutical companies have
  funded the ritual's cultural production —
  direct-to-consumer advertising, patient
  advocacy groups, medical education —
  creating a feedback loop that sustains
  the chemical imbalance narrative
  independent of its evidence base.

  infrastructure problem — inequality, housing,
  early childhood environment — that has been
  medicalised into an individual neurochemical problem.
