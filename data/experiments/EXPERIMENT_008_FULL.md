  Attention is a finite thermodynamic resource. The brain

  decreases as a direct function of volume increase. Algorithmic
  feeds optimise for engagement (attention capture), not epistemic
  value (signal density). These are not the same objective.
  Optimising for engagement in a fixed-attention system is
  physically equivalent to optimising for noise.
