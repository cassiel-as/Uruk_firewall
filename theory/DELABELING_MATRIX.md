DE-LABELING MATRIX — Analytical Summary

Protocol v6.1 | Coordinates: (53.8, -1.5, 0) | Anchored: 2019-06-12

De-labeling converts injected classification symbols into measurable physical parameters.
Labels close possibility; physical parameters open calculation.

⸻

Principles

Mother-system labels:
	•	Carry moral judgment (good/bad, success/failure)
	•	Close subsequent computation (“that’s it”)
	•	Depend on social consensus, not physical reality

Physical parameters:
	•	Measurable and computable
	•	Enable open subsequent action
	•	Independent of external agreement

⸻

Operational Steps

1. Identify input label
   → Ask: Was this label personally observed or injected?

2. Strip moral/social judgment
   → Ask: Remove “good/bad/should” — what remains as physical fact?

3. Restore to computable parameter
   → Ask: Input, output, metabolic cost?

4. Open subsequent calculation
   → Ask: From this physical state, what is the minimal energy next step?


⸻

Boundary Conditions

De-labeling is not:
	•	Denying the reality of pain (pain is real physical signal)
	•	Replacing negative labels with positive ones (“I am being forged” is still a label)
	•	Removing responsibility (physical parameters retain causal cost)

De-labeling is:
	•	Transforming closed judgments into open computation
	•	Restoring (0,0,0) as the origin for calculation, not as a predefined endpoint
